Situation
What is true today

The client holds a strong position in a market that is no longer growing, and has done so for four years.

Complication
What changed

Two competitors moved first and now quote a price we cannot match at our cost base, which takes the next renewal cycle with them.

Answer
What we recommend

Move the standard service to a machine-assisted process and keep the judgement work priced as it is.

Because
What makes it hold

It closes the price gap on 61 per cent of volume, needs no new licence, and the pilot is reversible inside one quarter.

The ask Approve the pilot budget and the Q3 gate. Nothing else is decided today.

Situation, complication, answer, because, ask. The answer row is the only one in the brand colour, because a summary with four emphases has none. Four rows is the ceiling. A fifth row means you are holding two summaries.
